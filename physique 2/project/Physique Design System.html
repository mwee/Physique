Physique is a premium, dark-first logbook for serious lifters. The system is quiet by default and loud only where it matters — the weight you moved, the record you broke, the trend that says you're due for a PR.

01

Principles

Speed first
Logging a set is under three taps. Numbers pre-fill from history; the keypad is one thumb-reach away. Chrome recedes so data leads.
Quiet luxury
Near-black surfaces, hairline borders, one confident accent. No gradients in chrome, no decoration that isn't earning its place.
Progress is the point
Estimated 1RM, volume and plateau signals are surfaced without asking. Records are celebrated in gold — the only warm note in the palette.
